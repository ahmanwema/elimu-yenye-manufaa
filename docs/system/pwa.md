## Caching policy (deliberately minimal)

- Precached: `/offline` and two icons. Nothing else.
- Navigations go network-first; the offline page is shown **only** when the network fails.
- **Never cached:** authenticated pages, API/Server Action responses, RSC payloads, media, or anything user- or tenant-specific. This is why `sw.js` handles navigations only and never calls `cache.put` at runtime.
